# TransFloat FAQ

### How do I translate a conversation?

Choose microphone translation and your source and target languages. Allow microphone access when prompted, then start listening. Language availability can depend on the device, iOS version, and installed language resources.

### Why don't subtitles appear in another app?

Cross-app translation uses the iOS system broadcast. Make sure the broadcast is running and the source app is playing audio. Some apps and protected content do not make usable audio available to the broadcast.

### Can I keep subtitles on screen as I switch apps?

TransFloat Pro supports floating subtitles through Picture in Picture. You can choose bilingual, original-only, or translation-only text in Subtitle Display settings.

### Can I save the subtitles?

Open a saved translation task and use its export option. You can export SRT for subtitles with timing or TXT for plain text.

### Which features require Pro?

Cross-app translation, audio file translation, floating subtitles, and optional iCloud sync require Pro. Check the app for current plans and prices.

### How do I get help?

Email [support@qinfu-cloud.cn](mailto:support@qinfu-cloud.cn). Include your app version, iOS version, device model, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Keep private audio and purchase receipts out of public issues.
